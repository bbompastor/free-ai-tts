3. Scriptwriting & NLE Timeline Workflow for Seamless Retakes

When working with metered cloud TTS tools, fixing a single mispronounced word or tightening a transition sentence costs extra character credits. Because PureTTS runs locally on your GPU/CPU with zero limits, you can adopt an iterative "Script-by-Scene" workflow:

  • Write for the Ear, Not the Eye: Replace parenthetical asides with em-dashes (—) so the neural duration predictor inserts a natural 250ms conversational beat instead of rushing through clauses.
  • Export Scene-by-Scene 24kHz WAVs: For long documentaries, synthesize each chapter or scene into its own uncompressed .wav file. Dropping discrete scene WAVs onto your DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, or CapCut timeline makes it effortless to align B-roll cuts and J-cuts.
  • Normalize Dialogue to -14 LUFS: Apply a gentle high-pass filter at 80 Hz, a subtle 2:1 compressor, and normalize integrated loudness to -14 LUFS (with a true peak ceiling of -1.0 dBTP) while ducking background music to -26 LUFS.
