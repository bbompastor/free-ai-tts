Why Creators Are Switching from ElevenLabs to PureTTS

ElevenLabs produces impressive speech synthesis, but their aggressive character pricing and restrictive tiers can quickly derail your budget. A single 20-minute YouTube video or a single chapter of an audiobook can easily consume 20,000 to 30,000 characters—exhausting your entire monthly allocation in minutes.

PureTTS takes a fundamentally different engineering approach: rather than processing your voiceover on costly GPU cloud clusters and billing you for every character, PureTTS runs the neural AI model (Kokoro-82M v1.0) directly on your device's graphics card (WebGPU) and multi-core CPU (WebAssembly). Because our hosting costs are zero, we never need to cap your characters, track your usage, or charge you a subscription.

🔒 Absolute Privacy

Lawyers, doctors, researchers, and enterprises cannot upload proprietary documents or patient notes to third-party cloud APIs. PureTTS operates in a secure, sandboxed browser tab where zero bytes of your text or audio ever touch a server.

⚡ Zero Server Latency

No waiting in cloud queue lines or facing rate-limit throttling during peak hours. Once model weights are cached in your browser (~85 MB), synthesis begins instantly with multi-chunk parallel execution.

🎧 Studio WAV Export

Don't settle for lossy, compressed MP3 files. PureTTS outputs pristine, 24,000Hz 16-bit uncompressed WAV files ready for direct import into Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Audacity, or Final Cut.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PureTTS really completely free?

Yes, 100%. There are no hidden trials, no "credit packs," and no credit card requirements. The application runs entirely client-side using your computer's computing power.

How does PureTTS voice quality compare to ElevenLabs?

PureTTS is powered by Kokoro-82M v1.0, an open-weight model trained on high-fidelity studio datasets. It achieves exceptional natural cadence, human-like breath pacing, and emotional warmth across multiple male and female American and British voices.

Can I convert long documents and books?

Yes! PureTTS features automatic sentence-level chunking and continuous WAV concatenation, allowing you to synthesize articles, multi-page PDFs, and book chapters without hitches.

Can I use the audio for monetized YouTube videos?

Yes. The Kokoro model weights are released under Apache 2.0. You own all audio generated through PureTTS and are free to use it for monetized YouTube videos, podcasts, and commercial products.
