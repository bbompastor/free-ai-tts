🌐 About PureTTS

Accessible, Unlimited & 100% Private AI Speech Synthesis for Everyone

Why we built PureTTS to run entirely in your browser without accounts, character caps, or cloud data collection.

Our Mission

Natural-sounding text-to-speech is more than a convenience—it is a foundational accessibility and productivity tool for millions of students, researchers, independent creators, and neurodivergent readers around the world. Yet almost every commercial TTS and PDF reader platform restricts free users to robotic legacy voices or cuts off reading after just a few pages unless they pay $15 to $139+ per year.

PureTTS was created to prove a better model is possible. By engineering an isolated Web Worker synthesis pipeline around the open-weight Kokoro-82M neural network, ONNX Runtime Web, and Mozilla PDF.js, PureTTS runs 100% inside the user's own browser. Because our servers never have to run expensive cloud GPU inference on your text, we can offer unlimited studio-grade voice generation at zero cost forever.

Who We Built PureTTS For

  • Students, Academics & Lifelong Learners: Convert dense multi-page PDF research papers, textbook chapters, and lecture notes into clear audio tracks that can be listened to offline while commuting or exercising.
  • Accessibility & Screen-Fatigue Relief: Provide readers with dyslexia, ADHD, visual impairments, or chronic eye strain with warm, human-inflected voices rather than fatiguing mechanical synthesizers.
  • Independent Creators, Educators & Game Developers: Generate royalty-free, commercial-ready 24kHz WAV voiceovers for YouTube tutorials, e-learning courses, documentary essays, and indie game prototypes under the permissive Apache 2.0 license.
  • Privacy-Conscious Professionals: Synthesize internal memos, legal briefs, and draft manuscripts with the mathematical certainty that zero bytes of text are ever transmitted over the network.

Open-Source Technology & Acknowledgments

PureTTS stands on the shoulders of the open-source AI and web standards communities:

  • Kokoro-82M v1.0 (hexgrad/kokoro): Open-weight 82-million-parameter neural TTS model licensed under Apache 2.0.
  • Transformers.js & ONNX Runtime Web: Enables hardware-accelerated WebGPU (`fp32`) and SIMD WebAssembly (`q8`) neural network execution directly in standard browsers.
  • Mozilla PDF.js: Client-side PDF parsing and page-range text extraction with automatic hyphenation and line-break cleanup.
