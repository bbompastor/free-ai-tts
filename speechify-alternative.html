Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Speechify so expensive compared to PureTTS?

Speechify runs neural inference on centralized cloud GPU servers and spends heavily on paid celebrity voice licensing and advertising, passing a $139/year cost onto users. PureTTS runs open-weight Kokoro-82M directly on your device's GPU/CPU via WebGPU and WebAssembly, eliminating server compute bills completely.

Does PureTTS downgrade to robotic voices after a certain number of words?

Never. There is no word meter, no daily timer, and no voice downgrade. Your 100,000th word uses the exact same studio-grade Kokoro-82M neural voice as your first word.

Which PureTTS voice is best for long textbook and article reading?
