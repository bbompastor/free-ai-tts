Privacy Policy

Effective Date & Last Updated: October 23, 2026

Summary of Our Privacy Architecture: PureTTS is engineered as a 100% client-side web application. Any text you type, paste, or import (including PDF, TXT, and Markdown files) and any audio you generate are processed exclusively inside your own device's memory using WebGPU and WebAssembly. We never upload, store, log, or inspect your text inputs or synthesized audio files on any server.

1. 100% Client-Side Text & Audio Processing

Unlike traditional cloud-based text-to-speech platforms that transmit your scripts to remote GPU servers for processing, PureTTS executes the open-weight Kokoro-82M neural model and Mozilla PDF.js parser directly inside your web browser sandbox:

  • Zero Text Uploads: The text you enter into the editor or extract from local PDF documents never leaves your device.
  • Zero Audio Recording or Retention: Synthesized 24kHz WAV audio buffers are generated in your browser's local RAM (`AudioContext` and `Blob` URLs) and are immediately cleared when you close or refresh the browser tab.
  • Suitability for Sensitive Content: Because no document data is transmitted over the network during speech generation, PureTTS preserves complete confidentiality for personal study notes, research drafts, and internal documents.

2. Local Browser Storage & Neural Model Caching

When you run speech synthesis for the first time, your browser downloads the public, open-weight neural model files (~45 MB to ~85 MB depending on whether you select FP32 WebGPU or 8-bit Quantized WebAssembly mode) from public content delivery networks (such as Hugging Face / jsDelivr CDN).

To prevent redundant downloads and enable offline functionality, your browser stores these read-only neural model weights in its standard Cache Storage API or Origin Private File System (OPFS). You can clear these cached model weights at any time through your browser's normal "Clear Site Data / Cached Images and Files" settings.

3. Cookies, Analytics, and Third-Party Advertising

To keep PureTTS 100% free with no subscriptions, no accounts, and no character limits, we use aggregated traffic analytics and display third-party advertisements and partner recommendations:

  • Google AdSense & DoubleClick Cookie: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to PureTTS or other websites on the internet.
  • Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve relevant advertisements to you. You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • Google Analytics (GA4): We use Google Analytics to measure anonymized, aggregate usage metrics (such as page views, device category, and browser compatibility) so we can optimize WebGPU and WebAssembly performance across desktop and mobile devices. We do not log or send any user-entered text to Google Analytics.
  • Affiliate & Partner Disclosure: For specialized capabilities that cannot run locally inside a browser (such as custom voice cloning or translation into 29+ non-English languages), PureTTS links to partner platforms such as ElevenLabs via affiliate tracking links (including Impact.com). If you choose to click a partner link and purchase a subscription on their platform, PureTTS may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

4. GDPR (European Economic Area) & CCPA (California) Rights

Because PureTTS does not require user accounts, email addresses, or personal profiles, we do not maintain a database of personally identifiable user accounts. Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EU/EEA, UK, and California), you have the right to:

  • Control or reject non-essential advertising and analytics cookies via your browser settings or consent preferences.
  • Request information regarding any third-party cookie identifiers processed by our advertising partners.
  • Clear all locally cached data stored by PureTTS at any time using your browser's site data controls.

5. Children's Privacy

PureTTS is a general-audience educational and creative productivity utility. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Because our tool requires no registration and transmits no user text to any backend server, no personal student data is collected when students use PureTTS to listen to study materials.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technical improvements, new browser APIs, or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, data protection inquiries, or technical feedback, please reach out via our official GitHub Repository & Support Tracker or visit our About & Documentation page.
