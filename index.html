How Browser-Based WebGPU & WebAssembly AI Voice Synthesis Works

Traditional text-to-speech platforms transmit your scripts to remote cloud GPU servers, which forces them to meter every character, require user accounts, and lock high-fidelity voices behind monthly subscriptions. PureTTS is engineered differently: it runs the entire 82-million-parameter Kokoro-82M v1.0 neural speech synthesis architecture directly inside an isolated browser Web Worker on your own computer or phone.

When you click Convert Text to Speech, PureTTS executes a four-stage acoustic pipeline locally in your device's memory:

1. Sentence & PDF Normalization

Long manuscripts, Markdown notes, and multi-page PDF documents are segmented along natural clause boundaries (~250–400 characters) while repairing split line-break hyphens so memory usage stays low and constant.

2. Phoneme (IPA) Tokenization

A Grapheme-to-Phoneme (G2P) engine translates English words, numbers, and punctuation into International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) phoneme sequences complete with lexical stress and pitch contour markers.

3. Local WebGPU / WASM Inference

ONNX Runtime Web executes the Kokoro StyleTTS2 + ISTFTNet neural network using either hardware-accelerated 32-bit floating-point (fp32) WebGPU compute shaders or multi-threaded 8-bit (q8) SIMD WebAssembly.

4. Lossless 24kHz WAV Mastering

Synthesized audio buffers stream to your browser's audio player in real time and concatenate into an uncompressed 24,000 Hz, 16-bit PCM .wav file ready for Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or Audacity.

Pro Tips for Directing Natural Pacing & Inflection

Because Kokoro-82M predicts prosody from syntax and punctuation, small edits to your script give you fine-grained control over emphasis and pauses:

  • Use Em-Dashes (—) for Conversational Beats: Placing an em-dash or semicolon mid-sentence creates a natural 250ms breath pause without dropping the speaker's vocal pitch the way a full period does.
  • Spell Out Ambiguous Abbreviations: Write technical units or initialisms phonetically (for example, twenty-four kilohertz instead of 24kHz, or A.I. with periods) when you want deliberate word stress.
  • Keep Sentences Under 40 Words: Shorter, well-punctuated sentences allow the neural duration predictor to place natural breath intakes between clauses. See our Voice Directing & WAV Mastering Handbook for complete examples.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PureTTS really 100% free with no character limits?

Yes! Traditional TTS websites pay expensive cloud GPU server bills for every conversion, forcing them to meter characters and charge subscriptions. Because PureTTS runs directly on your computer or phone's local processor via WebGPU and WebAssembly, our server compute costs are zero—allowing us to keep this service completely free and unlimited forever.

Is my text and audio data completely private?

Completely. Unlike cloud-based TTS services, PureTTS never uploads or transmits your text or PDF files to any external server. Every stage of text normalization, phoneme tokenization, and WAV audio synthesis happens inside your local browser sandbox.

Can I use the downloaded WAV audio commercially on YouTube or in audiobooks?

Yes. The underlying Kokoro-82M neural model is released under the permissive Apache 2.0 open-source license. You retain full commercial rights to use your generated audio in monetized YouTube videos, podcasts, audiobooks, indie video games, e-learning courses, and client projects without attribution requirements.

Why does the very first generation take a few seconds to initialize?

On your first run, your browser downloads the open-weight neural model files (~86 MB in CPU/Mobile mode or ~326 MB in full FP32 WebGPU Studio mode) and caches them locally in your browser's Cache Storage. Every subsequent visit loads the model directly from your local disk in milliseconds and even works offline.

What is the difference between WebGPU Studio Mode and CPU Safe Mode (WASM)?

WebGPU Studio Mode (fp32) executes unquantized 32-bit floating-point tensors directly on your graphics card for maximum dynamic fidelity and up to 10x faster-than-real-time synthesis. CPU Safe Mode (WASM q8) uses an 8-bit quantized model (~86 MB) that runs smoothly on any multi-core CPU across Safari, Firefox, Chrome, iOS, and Android without requiring a dedicated GPU.

Can I convert multi-page PDF textbooks and research papers to audio?

Yes! You can drag and drop any searchable .pdf, .txt, or .md file directly into the text box above, or use our dedicated PDF to Speech Converter to extract specific page ranges and automatically clean up broken line hyphens and page headers.
