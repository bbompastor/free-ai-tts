Frequently Asked Questions

Is this really 100% free with no limits?

Yes! Traditional TTS websites pay expensive cloud server bills for every conversion, forcing them to charge you. Because PureTTS runs directly on your computer or phone's local processor, our hosting costs are zero, allowing us to keep this service completely free and unlimited forever.

Is my data private?

Completely. Unlike cloud-based TTS services, we never transmit your text to any external server. Everything is synthesized locally in your browser memory.

Can I use the downloaded audio commercially?

Yes. The underlying Kokoro model is licensed under Apache 2.0. You are free to use your generated audio in YouTube videos, podcasts, educational materials, and commercial projects.

Why does the first generation take a few seconds?

On your very first use, your browser downloads the model weights (~45 MB to ~85 MB) and stores them in your browser's local cache. Subsequent generations load instantly and work even without an internet connection.
