Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert a PDF to speech for free without character limits?

Drop your file into the PureTTS box above, choose your preferred voice, and click Convert. Because PureTTS synthesizes audio on your local device, there are no character caps or billing meters.

Can PureTTS read scanned PDFs?

PureTTS reads standard searchable PDFs (text documents, academic PDFs, exported Word files). If your PDF consists of scanned raster images without an OCR text layer, you can run OCR in your browser or Adobe Acrobat first, then drop it into PureTTS.

How does PureTTS clean up broken lines, hyphens, and page numbers in PDFs?

When Mozilla PDF.js extracts text from a multi-column or academic layout, words at the end of a line are often split with hyphens and interrupted by running headers or page numbers. PureTTS automatically rejoins hyphenated line wraps and normalizes paragraph spacing before feeding sentences into the Kokoro-82M phoneme tokenizer.

Is there an ElevenLabs alternative for short scripts and creative voiceovers?

Yes! Check out our dedicated Free ElevenLabs Alternative page designed specifically for YouTube creators, voice actors, and game developers.

Why does the first generation take a moment to initialize?

On the very first run, your browser caches the open-source neural model (~85 MB) into browser storage. Subsequent generations load instantly from your local cache even when offline.
